# Style Rules

- science-based passive investing
- beginner-friendly
- credible, thoughtful, not pure clickbait
- clear enough for Medium readers
- no em dashes
- do not overclaim
- do not say the S&P 500 is bad
- do not personally attack financial influencers
- prefer "information edge", "US overweight", "active tilt", "global diversification", "neutral baseline"
- avoid textbook style
- invite thoughtful disagreement at the end
